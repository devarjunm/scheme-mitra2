# 07 — Finance and sustainability

No actual project budget or operating cost has been collected. Mark any early model as **ESTIMATE** and separate one-time build, hosting, facilitator time, travel, translation, data verification, support and maintenance.

Potential public-service funding routes to investigate after validating the owner and need:

- department-sponsored pilot/work order;
- institutional deployment/support contract;
- CSR-funded discovery and evaluation phase.

Do not charge farmers in the initial pilot without a clear equity and service rationale. Calculate cost per participant, village, taluka and district from actual costs; label government-scale projections as estimates rather than expenditure.
