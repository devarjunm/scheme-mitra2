Delete your account

You can request permanent deletion here without opening the mobile app.

This action is permanent. The live service will remove your account, farm profile, saved schemes, checklist states, application tracker, feedback, survey entries, product events and active sessions. Scheme records are not user data and are retained. Copies in backups follow the operator's published backup-retention schedule.

1. Sign in

Use your Scheme Mitra email and password. Your credentials are sent only to this service over its same-origin session.

2. Confirm deletion

Signed in as your account. Enter your password again and type DELETE.
