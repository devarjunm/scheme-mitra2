# Proposed pilot plan (not an approved partnership)

## Objective

Test whether a source-linked, explainable shortlist and staged checklist helps farmers reach a relevant official next step more clearly than their current process.

## Stage 0 — validation (before recruiting a pilot)

- Confirm a local problem with structured interviews.
- Identify a proposed Agriculture Department owner and verifier.
- Have all seeded scheme records reviewed and signed off.
- Complete privacy, consent, language, accessibility and safety review.
- Define a control/comparison approach that is ethical and practical.

## Stage 1 — usability (target up to 10 participants)

- Observe the same scheme-discovery task before and after assisted use.
- Record time, comprehension, missing-document identification and next-step clarity.
- Provide a facilitator and official escalation route; do not handle government credentials.
- Fix confusing wording before expanding.

## Stage 2 — bounded cohort (target up to 50, only after Stage 1)

- Include an explicit recruitment method, participant profile and consent process.
- Track relevance, data freshness, support time, errors and official-portal progression.
- Follow up only with consent; do not infer approval from a click.

## Stage 3 — one proposed taluka, then district

Proceed only if the source verifier and service owner agree, data can be maintained, support cost is understood, and observed outcomes justify expansion.

## Pilot gates

- **Accuracy gate:** no unresolved high-risk eligibility text; stale records can be withdrawn.
- **Usability gate:** participants understand that a match is not approval and can find the official next step.
- **Equity gate:** test Marathi, low literacy, older devices and assisted use.
- **Privacy gate:** only collect the minimum necessary fields and define retention/deletion.
- **Cost gate:** actual unit and support costs are documented.
