# 04 — Impact and measurement

No impact figures are asserted. Measure a small number of service outcomes with a real cohort:

- Time to identify a relevant scheme (baseline vs assisted journey).
- Share of participants who can explain why a scheme appeared.
- Share who can identify missing documents and the official next step.
- Recommendation usefulness, with reasons for non-relevance.
- Click-through to the official portal and, only with consent/follow-up, application progression.
- Farmer/staff effort and direct cost per participant.

Use the same task and consistent observation method before and after. Report denominator, sample, geography, period, missing data and any selection bias. A portal click is not an approved application or a benefit delivered.
