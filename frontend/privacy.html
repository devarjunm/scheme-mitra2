Privacy notice

Draft for operator review · Updated 8 October 2026

Release blocker: Before public launch, the service operator must add its registered/legal name, a monitored support/privacy contact, the production hosting and processor details, and the exact backup-retention period. This page intentionally does not invent those details.

Scheme Mitra is an independent information service. It is not a government portal and is not affiliated with MahaDBT or a government department. It provides preliminary, source-linked scheme information; it does not submit applications or make official eligibility decisions.

Information the service handles

  • Account information: email address, display name and a password hash. The service does not store your password in readable form.
  • Farm profile information you choose to enter: state, district, taluka/village, approximate land area, crop, farm category, support preferences and optional checklist states.
  • Saved scheme records, user-entered application-tracker notes/statuses, and optional feedback or pilot-survey responses.
  • Basic first-party product events needed to operate and improve the service. Do not enter names, Aadhaar numbers, bank details, or other sensitive information in free-text fields.
Data minimisation: Scheme Mitra does not request or store Aadhaar numbers, bank-account numbers, document images, or DigiLocker credentials. Checklist states are self-reported; the service does not validate document contents.

Why information is used

Account and profile data are used to sign you in, save your choices and compare the information you provide with the currently published rule records. Product events are used for service operations and measured product usage. They are not proof of farmer impact or scheme approval.

Where information is processed

Production hosting, database, email/support, and backup providers have not yet been selected in this workspace. The public operator must identify each provider and its role here before launch. The Android client stores its bearer session token using the platform-backed secure-storage facility; web sessions use secure cookies when deployed over HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

You may request deletion from the signed-in app or use the external account-deletion page. Deletion requires password confirmation. The live application removes account-linked profile, checklist, saved, tracker, feedback, event and session data. Encrypted backup copies, if any, may remain until the operator's stated backup-retention period expires; that period must be published before release.

Security

The service is being prepared for HTTPS, access controls, encrypted infrastructure and database backups. The production domain, deployment, independent security review and provider configuration are not yet confirmed. Do not use this draft service for high-risk or sensitive records.

Children and contact

The initial service is designed for adult farmers and account holders. A monitored operator privacy/support contact and a process for privacy requests must be supplied before the service is made public.
