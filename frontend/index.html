Important note

Guidance, not a decision

Scheme Mitra is an independent demo. It is not part of MahaDBT or any government department. Seed records link to claimed official sources but remain unverified until independent human review and publication; a recorded date is not sign-off. A profile match is not eligibility or approval. The demo does not submit applications, verify uploaded documents, or receive official application status.

This demo stores account email and farm-profile data. Use only fictional or test details. Never enter real Aadhaar or bank details or upload real documents; secure document storage and independent privacy/security review are not in place.
